Travels of India

Explore the timeless beauty of the Indian subcontinent

Taj Mahal

Located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, this white marble masterpiece was built by 22,000 workers. It is a [UNESCO World Heritage Site](https://whc.unesco.org) and a global symbol of love.

Qutub Minar

This historic monument is located in Mehrauli, New Delhi. Standing at 73 metres, it is the tallest brick minaret in the world.(https://delhitourism.gov.in).

Hawa Mahal

Known as the "Palace of Winds," this pink sandstone structure is located in Jaipur, Rajasthan. It features 953 small windows called Jharokhas. Discover more at [Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in).

Assam

Assam is one of the part of the seven sisters.In this state you'll see a speacial dance done on special festivals,atleast 3 times a year . This state is really famous for it's tea estates.(https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in).
